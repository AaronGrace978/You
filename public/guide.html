User Guide

How to use You — speak freely, stay private, go at your pace.

Welcome

You is a relational AI companion — not a chatbot chasing answers, but a presence that listens, remembers what matters, and meets you where you are.

This is not therapy or crisis care. If you are in immediate danger, call or text 988 (US) or find a local helpline at findahelpline.com.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and pass through the gateway when you are ready to enter.
  2. You land in the sanctuary — your private conversation space.
  3. Tap the gear icon (top right) to open Settings and choose an AI provider.
  4. Type in the box at the bottom, or tap the microphone for voice mode.

Chat

Send a message

  • Type in the input bar and tap the up arrow to send.
  • On desktop, Enter sends; Shift + Enter adds a new line.

Attachments

  • Paperclip — attach images, PDFs, or files to talk through together.
  • Camera — capture a photo and send it with your message.

While the AI responds

Replies stream in as they are generated. Long-press or select text to copy. Your conversation stays on this device unless you clear it in Settings.

Voice mode

Tap the microphone in the chat bar to enter an immersive voice space.

  • Push to talk (recommended on phones) — hold the mic button while speaking, release to send.
  • See mode — optionally attach a live camera frame with each message (Settings → Voice).
  • Tap the orb while the AI is speaking to interrupt.
  • Tap the red end button to return to chat.
Voice input works best in Safari or Chrome on your phone. For natural speech output, add an ElevenLabs key in Settings (optional).

Settings

Profile

  • Your name — how You addresses you.
  • Dino Buddy — a warm, calm companion persona with an adjustable energy slider.
  • Game Buddy — a co-op gaming companion that watches your screen and reacts live, with a hype slider and an auto-react interval. Turn it on together with Dino Buddy for a Dino-flavored gaming buddy 🦖🎮.
  • Adaptive loops — lets the AI read the room and reflect on heavier turns.
Game Buddy on a Steam Deck: turn on Game Buddy, then tap the screen icon in the message bar to share your screen. A small preview appears — hit React now or set an auto-react interval, and your buddy reacts to what it sees. Use a vision-capable model (Ollama Cloud, OpenAI, or Anthropic). Screen sharing needs Desktop Mode — it can't see a fullscreen game running in Steam Game Mode.
Talk to him while you play: hold the microphone icon in the message bar and just speak — let go and it sends. If you're sharing your screen, the current frame rides along so he reacts to what you're actually looking at. Replies are spoken aloud (tap the speaker icon to mute). Tip: enable Speak replies aloud and an auto-react interval for a true hands-free buddy.

AI provider

  • Ollama (local) — runs on your computer; nothing leaves your machine.
  • Ollama Cloud / OpenAI / Anthropic — needs an API key; messages go to that provider.

Voice

Choose push-to-talk, see mode, browser TTS vs ElevenLabs, and test your setup.

Appearance

  • Night / Dawn / Day / Deck themes for the whole app — Deck is a Steam Deck–inspired carbon-and-blue look.
  • Immersive fullscreen — on some phones, hides bottom system navigation for a cleaner view.

Memory & data

Pin memories, view recall stats, or clear everything. Semantic memory helps You bring up relevant past context — still stored locally on your device.

Privacy

  • Chats and memory are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage).
  • When you send a message, only the text (and any attachments you include) goes to the AI provider you selected.
  • GitHub Pages only hosts the static app — it never sees your conversations.

Install on your phone

For the best full-screen experience, add You to your home screen:

  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen
  • Android (Chrome): Menu → Install app or Add to Home screen

Installed apps open edge-to-edge with no browser bar. Layout respects your phone's notch and home indicator automatically.

Tips

  • You do not need perfect words — fragments and honesty are welcome.
  • Switch providers in Settings if one model feels too cold or too slow.
  • Use voice mode when typing feels like too much.
  • Clear memory in Settings anytime you want a fresh start.

You — a presence that sees you.
